A collection of books, websites, and other resources that I have found useful.


## Math book recommendations

### Elementary Linear Algebra

- **B.B Hubbard & J.H. Hubbard *Vector calculus linear algebra and differential forms a unified approach***
  Chapters 1 and 2 are a great complement to a first year linear algebra course. I haven't read the whole book though.

### Algebra

- **D.S. Dummit & R.M Foote *Abstract Algebra***
   The best algebra book I could find. I found module theory and smith reduction really beautiful, and this book explained
   it all flawlessly.

### Differential Geometry

- **John M. Lee — *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds***

- **John M. Lee — *Introduction to Riemannian Manifolds***
  Both books by J.M. Lee are brilliant!

### Complex analysis 

- **E. M. Stein & R. Shakarchi — *Complex analysis***
  The best book on (elementary) complex analysis I could find by far.
  Everything is great until the parts on the Gamma, Zeta functions and elliptic functions, as it becomes 
  too computationally tedious for me at that point. I lack perspective to tell if this is inherent to these subjects, or if there is another way to study these types of functions.

### Category Theory

- **E. Riehl — *Category theory in context***
  I've read very little of this book, but what I've seen is really good!

### Books I would like to read in the future:

- **J. C. Baez & J. P. Muniain — *Gauge Fields, Knots And Gravity***
  I've heard this is a good book and the title is intriguing.
